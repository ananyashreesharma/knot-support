Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026. Applies to Knot on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Knot does not collect any data, except a message you choose to send us.

  • Everything you enter in Knot (your habit, the days you log, your reminder time and settings) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Knot has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Feedback you send: if you use "Talk to me", your message and the email address you enter, along with the app's version and your device's system version (to help fix problems), are sent through the form service Formspree to the developer's inbox, only so we can reply. They are never used for anything else, never shared, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS or Android. Knot only asks for permission to show notifications if you turn reminders on.
  • If Knot asks you to rate it, the rating screen is Apple's App Store or Google Play's. Knot never sees your rating.
  • Your data is included in your device's own backups: on iPhone and iPad, iCloud or computer backups under Apple's privacy policy; on Android, Android's backup to your Google account if you have it turned on, under Google's privacy policy.
  • You can export your history or erase everything at any time in Knot's Settings.
