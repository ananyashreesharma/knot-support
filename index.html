Knot

The habit tracker with no streaks to break. Miss a day and nothing breaks; come back and your thread ties a knot.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Open a request here and we'll reply.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Knot works entirely on your device.
  • What happens if I miss a day? Nothing breaks. When you come back, your thread ties a knot.
  • Do I log rest days? No. Planned rest days fill in on their own.
  • How do reminders work? Turn them on in Knot's Settings. One nudge, only on planned days, only if today isn't logged.
  • Can I keep my history? Yes. Settings → Export your thread saves every day as a spreadsheet.
